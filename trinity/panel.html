Trinity

Trinity is who you talk to. UltRix is the algorithm behind these picks, and she answers only from what it has actually recorded.

What the system does

It builds its own fair price for a market before it reads what that market costs, applies a fixed set of bars in code, and cards only what clears every one of them unaided.

How to read an answer

Every figure in an answer is read or computed from its own records. It will not estimate, and when its records cannot answer a question it says so.

What you can ask

  • today's card, and the bar each pick cleared
  • why a side was not picked, and the exact bar that refused it
  • the record over a window, with its closing-line value
  • how a league, a market class or a price band has done
  • what was reachable on a day and never scored
  • a league model's verdict, and what would have to change for that league to card
  • how the bars, the ladder and the gates work, and what the objective is

What she will not do

  • anything in money terms: what is staked, held or earned
  • how sure it is, or anything it is reasoning about and has not published
  • what to bet. She will show the record and leave the decision with you
  • a prediction for a game the algorithm never carded
  • how the system is operated internally

The objective is the win rate on graded picks over a rolling twelve months, read from its public record.
